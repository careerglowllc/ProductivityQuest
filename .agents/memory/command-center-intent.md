---
name: Command Center intent
description: Product intent and privacy boundary for macro-goal strategy journaling.
---

Command Center is high-level strategy journaling for macro goals, with a detailed "Star Command" debrief feel rather than reflective prose. Personal business notes are unique to the designated account, not shared example content.

**Why:** The user requested visual business branches, allocation charts, decision flows, and pros/cons, and explicitly confirmed that their information and journaling are account-specific.

**How to apply:** Preserve the strategic, decision-oriented treatment in future entries. Keep personal source material private. Distinguish supplied facts and targets from illustrative allocation models; never present invented allocations as measured results.

The project's GitHub repository was confirmed public. Server-only source files and uploaded attachments are not a privacy boundary if committed and pushed there.

**Why:** A server-only briefing would still reveal personal business notes through public Git history.

**How to apply:** Keep personal briefing files out of Git, including unpublished parent commits. Use authenticated, owner-checked imports into account storage rather than code-based personal seeds.
